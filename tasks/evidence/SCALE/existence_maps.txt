Reading: the bounded parts are caches; what grows without bound is ~63 B per node (+ the same per rel)
in every open shard, and a clean open that walks every node key to fill them (~1.4 s per 1 M nodes).

At the SCALE goal (2 TB a day, ~4 billion 500-byte facts):
  - a day-shard's maps: 4e9 x 63 B ~ 250 GB of heap while it is open (hot and warm shards are open);
  - its clean reopen: ~4e9 / 1 M x 1.4 s ~ 90 min; a crash reopen (~10 s per 1 M, CHAIN) ~ 11 h;
  - the reference shard (ai-soc's Derived) is always open and grows for ever: both costs grow with
    the store's total, not with a window.
