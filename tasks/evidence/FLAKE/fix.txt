FLAKE (row 119 follow-up (a); branch flake), 2026-10-26
symptom: TestTemporalPropertySeekAnswersEverLabelMembers/badger missed the relabelled node at TxPin: pinBefore only
  beside other parallel tests (passed alone; it had been made serial).
cause (test): the fixture pinned with g.readNow(), which reserves nothing (context.go documents peekNow/readNow as
  "NOT a sound as-of pin"). A commit takes the wall millisecond whenever it is above the last stamp (c.now), so
  when the wall had moved past the floor before the pin, the pin and the relabel right after it read the same
  millisecond: the relabel was stamped at the pin and showed there. Alone, the fixture's burst of writes ran the
  floor ahead of the wall, so the next stamp was floor + 1, after the pin.
fix: the fixture pins with g.Temporal.NowTx() (reserves the instant; every later write is stamped after it); the
  test runs in parallel again. Not a product bug: a sound pin is NowTx or an instant a write returned.
red: TestTemporalPropertySeekPinUnderFrozenClock (a test clock that jumps past every stamp just before the pin and
  holds still) with the readNow pin: "at the pin before the relabel: [plain], want [plain relabelled]" on memory,
  memory-segments, badger, sharded; green with NowTx; -count=3 green.
