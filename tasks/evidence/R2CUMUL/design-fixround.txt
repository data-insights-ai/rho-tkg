1. THE RULE THIS ROUND APPLIES (one sentence, from AGENTS "examined and rejected entries count")
  Every entry a gather takes into its hands is one candidate of the read: held ahead before the work that produces it,
  charged by whoever drops it, charged by the reader when it is read. So for a read that runs to its end:
      Work.Candidates of the unlimited control == the smallest Candidates budget that answers        (invariant I)
  This is what M1 and L1 ask for together, and it has one consequence RH must confirm (section 8, risk 1): the
  "prune before refusing" promise of R2 late counts (01fd59cd) goes. A pruned ID that is charged uses the room it was
  meant to free, so no prune can rescue a list past the room. Refusal is on entries examined, on every backend.
  Not covered by I, as today: a door stopped by Limit or by fn (held entries it never read are not charged).

2. M3 DECISION: ONE PATH. GatherHold is removed; the reservation lives in ReadTally.
  File pkg/graph/store/read_budget.go (gather_hold.go deleted). Owner of the admission: the meter.
    func (t *ReadTally) Held() *ReadTally     // t with a hold scope (one per read). nil t, or no candidate limit: t.
    func (t *ReadTally) Hold(n int) error     // n entries about to be produced, counted later. Adds to the scope's
                                              // pending under its mutex; pending > CandidateRoom() refuses: the
                                              // pending entries are counted as work done (AddCandidates, or
                                              // stage.refuse on a staged tally), the error is kept and returned by
                                              // every later Hold. No scope: nil, nothing held (gatherFree).
    func (t *ReadTally) Drop(n int) error     // n held entries were examined and dropped: pending -= n, then
                                              // Candidates(n). No scope: Candidates(n).
  The room is read live (CandidateRoom), so candidates the store charged for real before (the listed door's current
  members) need no offset: that offset was H2. Staged() copies the scope pointer. CandidatesAhead stays as it is, for
  its single-set users (index/valid_end.go, badgerstore_label_lazy.go, the property window open).
  QueryOpts carries the scope beside meter and door (unexported field; ReadTally() binds it), set once per read.
  Why not keep GatherHold: it is a second counter with its own limit arithmetic (the source of H2), its refusal is
  made by a core closure (two owners of one count), and it is per call (the K-label hole). Why not let the store
  charge real candidates for its copy: the scans charge per entity when they read it; moving that charge to the
  gather changes Work.Candidates at every refusal of another dimension, for no gain in bound.
  Capability: TxMemberGatherMeteredCapability returns to (token, t, fn), the form before cbe00a63.
  Ownership after the change:
    candidates of a gather entry   held by its producer (store for a sidecar set, core for a listing or a fold ID),
                                   charged once: by the helper that drops it (Drop) or by the door that reads it
    BufferBytes of a copy          the store that makes it, before it (unchanged)
    ShardOpens                     tiered, before the open (unchanged)

8. RISKS
  1 The prune-before-refuse promise is withdrawn and sharded refuses reads it answered. RH's call. The alternative
    is a documented exception "envelope-pruned and pin-filtered IDs are free", which keeps M1's old refusal point
    only by giving up the refusal before the copy (R2-1 itself) and leaves L1 open.
  2 Work.Candidates moves for answered reads with a pin, After or a valid-time filter, and at refusals. Sigma and
    ai-soc tests that pin those figures need the list in 3.3, 4 and 5 before they move their rho pin.
  3 memory's Hold(len(set) - current) depends on every acquire door recording into the sidecar; T2 on memory
    guards it. badger's stale former entries hold high by their number (documented).
  4 R2-2 changes a wire scanner (storeutil): fail-closed behaviour and the fallback decode must give the same
    tokens; V3 compares against the RAM-mode store. Read docs/badger.md first.
  5 Tiered shards run in parallel: shards that held before the refusal still copy (T4 bounds it at the room).
  6 Paged temporal ByLabel pays the label's former members on every page (After drops are charged); with O1 and O3
    this is the next thing consumers will ask about.
