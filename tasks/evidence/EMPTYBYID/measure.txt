EMPTYBYID round 2: handles, allocations and time of the write doors that name a missing ID in a proven-empty shard's window,
before and after (14 Nov 2026, go1.26.9, linux/amd64, 32 CPU, load average 12-17 while measuring).

Question: the reads skip a closed shard proven empty (round 1); which writes still opened it?

Root cause: every write door that changes a row its ID already has (an update, a delete, a label change, a history trim) or
needs an endpoint to exist (a relationship create) resolved the ID's shard through the writable route
(routeNodeShard / routeRelShardFor, readOnly false). That route checks the shard of the ID's mint time out writable: a closed
cold shard is opened, its no-rows mark woken (cleared, the catalog saved before the write), only for the door to read "not
found". The open is transient (readTransientOpen): at check-in it closes again when no MaxOpenColdShards room is left, which
re-seals the counts and saves the catalog once more; with room it stays until the idle sweep, which closes an empty open shard
at once (openEmptyLocked), so the next miss opens it again. A miss cost an open of a badger handle (its memtable arena: about
100 MB of allocation at the default memtable size), a catalog save, and a close with a second save.

Fix: routeExisting (tieredstore_routing.go), the writable route that skips a shard proven empty the way routeRead does and
returns the open hot shard, which answers the door's own miss. A door that stores a row under the ID keeps routeWrite
(PutNodeVersion, PutRelVersion, the creates, ex-adjacency legs).

Measurement: pkg/graph/store/tiered/empty_by_id_write_measure_test.go (go test -tags measure -run
TestEmptyByID_MeasureWriteMisses; make measure). Fixture: UTC week shards, RelsByMint, no MaxOpenColdShards (so every
transient open closes at its check-in: one open per miss; with a cap the opens fall to one per idle sweep, as in round 1),
the first shard rotated out empty, demoted, closed and marked, two reference nodes and event nodes on the hot shard. One
round = the 24 write misses of byIDWriteProbes (ReplaceNode, DeleteNode, DeleteNodeCascade, DeleteNodesBatch x2, the four
label-token doors, ReplaceNodeWithHistory, DeleteNodeWithHistory, TruncateNodeHistory, ReplaceRelationship, DeleteRelationship,
UndoRelationshipCreate, DeleteRelationshipsBatch x2, ReplaceRelWithHistory, DeleteRelWithHistory, TruncateRelHistory,
PutRelationship x2 and PutRelationshipsBatch x2 with a missing endpoint) and then the idle sweep. 20 rounds after one
warm-up round; per round:
