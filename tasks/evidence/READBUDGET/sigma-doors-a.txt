What is counted (green):
  GetByIDsCtx    Candidates: one per ID, before any read. DecodedBytes: the stored bytes of each row decoded on a cache
                 miss, before the decode (badger prefetchRelTallied; sharded passes the tally to each shard; tiered reads
                 each shard through a metered shard read). ShardOpens (tiered): the archive and every closed event shard
                 the batch reaches, before the open (checkoutArchiveCharged, checkoutForReadCharged).
  HasHistoryCtx  Candidates: 1. ShardOpens (tiered): every closed shard the presence walk opens, before the open.
  LatestStampsCtx Candidates: 1. ShardOpens (tiered): the route of the current-row stamps, and the history walk including
                 the whole-chain fold of a history that spans shards (read through a metered snapshot, which counts decodes).
  A wrapper store embedding a native one refuses a DecodedBytes or ShardOpens budget ("unsupported") and, under a budget
  that bounds only Candidates, reads through its own overrides (the metered capabilities are taken only on an exact native
  store, c.storeCountsReads).
