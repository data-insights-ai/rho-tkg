What is counted (green), Rels().ByTypeAndPropertyCtx with a time coordinate:
  Candidates   each membership entry the index hands on, as the walk produces it and before the pin filter
               (forEachRelPropertyCandidateID's member callback; a membership kept on disk, RelPropertyIndexOnDiskTypes, is
               admitted as its fold grows through the tally, store.ReadTally.CandidateRoom/CandidatesAhead, on badger and on
               every tiered shard); the current lookup's matches (currentOpts carries the meter; the store counts them
               before its decode); on the fold (no usable index) each candidate the current lookup did not count, before it
               is resolved. 128 members of which one survives the pin: a budget of 10 refuses at the 11th entry on every
               backend (TestReadBudget_RelByTypeAndPropertyTimeCountsBeforePinFilter).
  HistoryExamined / DecodedBytes
               the current row, history skeleton and winning version of each candidate read through a metered read snapshot
               (meteredEntityReads; the rel twins findRelVersionForOptsRd, relAtLockedTxRd, findRelVersionMatchingDuringTxRd,
               relAsOfLockedRd: TxPin on badger and memory through store.TransactionTimeQueryMeteredCapability); the memory
               store counts a chain's versions when it reads them.
  ShardOpens   tiered: the membership fan-out (PropertyTxMemberMeteredCapability, ForEachRelPropertyTxMemberMetered), the current
               lookup's fan-out and the metered snapshot's checkouts, each closed shard before it opens. A fold with no usable
               index (the history-ID walk) cannot count its opens: on tiered under a ShardOpens budget it refuses before the
               walk, Limit "unsupported", as the node twin (TestReadBudget_ShardOpens_TieredFoldRefused).
  BufferBytes  24 per candidate-set entry (a duplicate entry is charged once), 24 per current match added, 8 per result row.
  A store that cannot count (a wrapper embedding a native store) refuses a DecodedBytes or ShardOpens budget ("unsupported",
  before any work) and answers under a Candidates-only budget through its own reads.
