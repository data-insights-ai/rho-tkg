design: a reader (checkoutStoreReadOnly and the read routings, switched in MOVEREAD) opens a closed cold shard
  read-only; it opens writable as before when the read-only open fails (an unclean WAL needs a writable recovery)
  or when the shard's index definitions differ from the anchor's (only a writable open syncs them;
  anchoredIndexesMatch). A checkout that may write (checkoutStore, checkoutStoreForRead) replaces a read-only
  handle: it gates new checkouts as a move does, waits for the handle's readers (its own pin excepted), closes it
  and opens writable (the directory lock is shared by the read-only handle). ColdOpens counts both opens.

  shard_index_fanout.go is for relationship temporal indexes), so a mismatch at a cold open only follows an
  interrupted DDL; the check stays (tested by indexes on the shard alone and on the anchor alone).
