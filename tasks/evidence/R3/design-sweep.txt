**1. Lifecycle**
- Hot: unchanged (MemTableSize). Reference: unchanged.
- Rotation hot->warm: the old hot's handle is closed and reopened writable at WarmMemTableSize via openBadgerStoreWithRecovery (tieredstore.go:1964 -> shardCfg:1977 -> nonHotCfg warm_memtable.go:46). Writable, not read-only: warm shards are owner shards and take updates of long-lived nodes (docs/persistence.md:461, :458 "open Badger handles stay writable"). Read-only warm with lazy upgrade is a later step, gated on a per-tier write counter from ai-soc's B0 run (B3 below records it). Only when WarmMemTableSize > 0.
- Demotion warm->cold (and hot->cold direct, catalog.go:118): the handle is closed (counts sealed, catalog saved, as closeForMove does). The next read opens it read-only (openColdReadOnlyLocked, lifecycle:60, ~3 MB), a late write upgrades via openColdWritableLocked (lifecycle:82) at warm size. This generalises RH02EMPTY (closeEmptyDemotedShardsLocked, lifecycle:625: empty shards only, never waits for a pin) to every demoted shard, waiting outside ts.mu.

- Why lock-free checkouts are safe: every non-cold fast path increments activeReqs and then tests es.moving (checkoutStore lifecycle:147-164, pinForRead :286-296, checkoutOpenStoreForRead :376-386) and backs off to waitMoveLocked (shard_move.go:228). The swap sets moving before reading activeReqs; either the reader is counted (swap waits) or it sees moving (reader waits). This is the invariant MoveShard relies on for warm shards today. Readers in flight keep their pin, so an old view stays valid until released; readSnapshot pins only the reference shard (tieredstore_read_snapshot.go:43-49), event reads are per-call pins. Writers to other shards never wait; writers to the swapping shard wait one swap. A hot promotion mid-swap (catalog.go:148, calendar windows) is bounded: the hot fast path checks moving (:158), writers wait one swap and resume on the reopened store.

**Risks not closed**
1. Swap duration grows with the shard (section D); the memtable is already flushed at rotation (catalog.go:51), so the remaining cost is the open image at Close. At ai-soc volumes a day shard's Close could take seconds and block that shard's owner writes for that long. Measure at 1-10 M rows before accepting; mitigation if needed: a skip above a size threshold.
2. The Close's flush and image write compete for IO/CPU with hot writes (throughput dip, not a lock stall). Measure on B0.
3. Warm write volume unknown: a heavy writer to an 8 MB warm arena makes more L0 tables (section B: no throughput loss at 100 KB rows; small rows unmeasured). 16 MB is the safer default suggestion.
4. A long-running change-log scope defers every swap until it ends (logged).
5. RSS gain is a hypothesis (finding 4) until B0.
