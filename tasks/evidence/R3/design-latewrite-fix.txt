=== 1. M1: decision ===
A  Grow: a small cold handle that has committed coldGrowBytes is closed by the handle sweep; the shard's next writable open
   takes MemTableSize.
   RAM: one 83.2 MiB arena (at 64 MiB) per cold shard under sustained writes, until IdleTimeout: what every cold writable
   handle cost before L1, so the bound is the pre-L1 one; nothing per entity. State: one atomic counter per open badger.Store,
   one bool per EventShard. The close comes before the open: never two arenas of one shard. Lifecycle: no new sweep action,
   no second cleanup path: the close is sweepClose (gate, pin drain, scope deferral, count seal, close fault, as today), the
   open is openColdLocked. One new handle fact, the bool.
B  Only late writes small, window shards at MemTableSize. Rejected: it does not reach the backfill (finding M1).
C  Document the opt-out only. RAM and lifecycle unchanged. Rejected: L1 changed every consumer's default, so the cost lands
   on consumers who set nothing, and the opt-out gives back the 83 MiB late write L1 removed. The line is written anyway.
D  Replace the handle inline in checkoutStore (openColdWritableLocked, the read-only upgrade). No bool. Rejected: a writable
   handle carries scope records and pending rows, whose replacement rules live in the sweep (AGENTS.md shard lifecycle).
Chosen: A. No question for René: the goal and the one-path rule decide it, and the threshold is derived from badger.
A, exactly
1  const coldGrowBytes = 4 * coldMemTableSize (32 MiB). badger compacts L0 at 5 tables (options.go:138 NumLevelZeroTables,
   rho keeps it): four flushes of 8 MiB stay in L0 unmerged (design-latewrite-floor.txt section 1: 60 MiB bulk at 8 MiB, 4-8
   tables, no throughput loss). ai-soc's late write (5,000 rows a day fixture) stays below it and keeps the 13.4 MiB open.
2  badger.Store: an atomic counter of the bytes its flushes committed on this handle (the pendingBytes the flush snapshots,
   badgerstore_flush.go:328, added after a successful commit; a staged flush counts once), with a reader, and a reader of
   the open's effective MemTableSize if none exists. Logical bytes, not heap.
3  One predicate ts.coldGrowOwedLocked(es) in warm_memtable.go (caller holds es.shardMu): ts.warmMemTableSize == 0, tier cold,
   es.store != nil, not es.storeRO, the handle's MemTableSize below hotMemTableSize, committed bytes >= coldGrowBytes. An
   explicit WarmMemTableSize is the consumer's word and never grows.
4  Who moves it:
   - checkoutStore, both cold writable branches (tieredstore_lifecycle.go:177-198, :206-235), under the shardMu they hold:
     when the predicate holds, ts.sweepPend(es) and ts.kickHandleSweep(). Two atomic loads, cold path only.
   - sweepActionLocked, TierCold: `!es.coldOpened || ts.coldGrowOwedLocked(es)` -> sweepClose. The comment at :217-221
     becomes: a late-write handle is left to IdleTimeout until it has committed coldGrowBytes; that close happens once per
     small handle, so steady late writes do not reopen on every kick.
   - sweepClose, where it closes under shardMu: es.growNext = the predicate's value read before the close (this also covers
     a cold window shard's first handle, which the sweep closes anyway).
   - openColdLocked passes es.growNext down to shardCfg / nonHotCfg (`case cold && !grow`) and clears it after a successful
     writable open. openColdReadOnlyLocked neither reads nor clears it.
   - EventShard.growNext bool, shardMu-protected, declared beside hotArena and coldOpened. Not persisted: after a restart
     the shard opens small and grows again after 32 MiB. A flag left on a shard nobody writes again costs one hot-size open
     at its next late write (the pre-L1 cost, once). The grown handle is coldOpened at the hot size: the predicate is
     false, the sweep owes nothing, IdleTimeout closes it, and the open after that is small again.

=== 4. CHANGELOG facts (the docs keeper writes them, under [Unreleased]) ===
P0 WALREPLAY
- Defect: a leftover WAL that badger's replay had cut to its content and a second crash left was sized by its file; an open
  below the size that wrote it (WarmMemTableSize, the sweep's reopen, a cold late write) ended the process in badger ("Arena
  too small"). Reproduced with 11 and 20 MiB of rows cut: exit 1; the uncut WAL opened.
- Fix: badger.Config.WALReplayMemTableSize (wiring field, valid [8 MiB, 1 GiB]; tiered sets its hot MemTableSize). Below it
  any leftover WAL is flushed first at max(that size, file size / 2). Cost: one open at the hot size (83.2 MiB arena at
  64 MiB) per shard with a leftover WAL, an empty one included, after a crash only. Not covered: MemTableSize itself lowered
  between runs plus the double crash. walTruncations counts a WAL longer than its content; an exactly cut WAL counts 0.
- Tests: TestWALReplay_CutWALOpensAtWarmSize, TestWALReplay_ColdLazyOpenOverHotSizeWAL (tiered);
  TestWALReplay_MigrateFlushesCutWALAtFloor, TestWALReplay_NewOpensCutWALBelowFloor, TestWALReplay_UncutWALAboveTwiceTheFloor.
R3-3 LATEWRITE (L1 and the grow)
- With WarmMemTableSize 0 a shard cold at its writable open (a late write into a closed day, beside its read-only handle, a
  cold window shard made on demand, Clear, the retention drop's rollback) opens at min(MemTableSize, 8 MiB). Warm window
  shards and every role-blind open keep MemTableSize; a read-only open keeps the hot size.
- Measured on ai-soc's config, 5,000 rows a day: late-write open 86.3 -> 13.4 MiB, +84.5 -> +11.8 live, close 7.3, a daily
  late write 93.4 -> 20.8 MiB (latewrite-green.txt).
- A small cold handle that has committed 32 MiB is closed by the handle sweep; the next writable open takes MemTableSize
  until the idle close. Figures: section 1 step 5, as measured (latewrite-fix-green.txt).
- Opt-out: WarmMemTableSize = MemTableSize; the sweep no longer swaps a handle at an equal size. A flush above 1.2 MiB on
  an 8 MiB handle is staged (format marker as docs/persistence.md states). Binary sizes in the touched docs are MiB.
- Tests: T1-T3 and T7-T9 by name, TestLateWriteMemtable_OnlyColdAtTheOpen, TestLateWriteColdShardOpensAtSmallMemtable.
