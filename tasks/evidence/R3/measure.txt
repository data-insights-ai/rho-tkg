R3 (René's RH list, 13 Nov): cheaper writable and inactive shards. Measured 13 Nov on rho-tkg at v4.107.1 + this branch,
host with other sessions running, Go 1.26.9, badger v4.9.2.

Method (pkg/graph/store/tiered/shard_memtable_measure_test.go, `go test -tags measure -v -run TestShardMemTableStates`;
R3_PER_DAY=n sets the rows per day, default 5,000): a tiered store (MemTableSize 64 MB, ShardWindow 24 h, ColdAfter 48 h,
MintAtValidFrom, a test clock moved a day at a time) takes 5,000 nodes a day over six day shards, then a backfill of
50 nodes into a past window (a window shard made on demand, cold), the idle sweep, a cold read, a late write into the
cold shard, Close, a restart, one more rotation. Per state: rw / ro = open writable / read-only handles among the event
shards (+ the reference shard in rw); live, sys, goal = HeapAlloc, Sys and NextGC after two GCs; alloc = TotalAlloc
during the step; peakAl, peakIn, peakSy, peakRSS = the peak HeapAlloc, HeapInuse, Sys and RSS (MB) sampled every 2 ms
during the step; step ms = wall time of the step (it includes the 5,000 writes of a "day" row). sys is the process
total and never falls; read the other columns.

Findings:
 1. Every writable handle holds 86.5 MB live at 64 MB: the arena is MemTableSize + maxBatchSize + maxBatchCount x 96
    = 83.2 MB (the consumer's figure), plus about 3 MB. At 8 MB it is 10.4 MB + 3 MB. A hot, warm, demoted-open or
    cold-writable shard costs the same: live heap = 86.5 MB x writable handles (days 1 to 6: 177, 264, 350, 437, 523, 610).
 2. WarmMemTableSize (in the tree) cuts the opens made in a non-hot role: a window shard made on demand (alloc 86.2 to
    13.4 MB at 8 MB, 23.8 at 16 MB), a cold shard a late write opens writable (alloc 90.1 to 17.3 MB; live 436.5 to
    363.9), and a restart (2 warm shards: live 345.7 to 200.3 MB, alloc 353.0 to 207.1, GC goal 691.7 to 400.9 MB).
    It does not touch the running process's hot to warm to demoted chain: those handles open hot and keep the 83 MB
    arena (day 6 live: 609.6 against 609.9).
 3. Prototype lifecycle variants (section C; the code is in proto_r3.go.txt, not in the tree) shrink that chain but
    cost more transient allocation and a stall under ts.mu.Lock at each rotation:
    - RO reopen of a demoted shard (close, then a read-only open): live per demoted-open shard 86.5 to ~3 MB (day 6:
      609.6 to 359.7, the idle sweep held off); each rotation allocs +10.9 MB (113.9 against 103.0) and peaks +11 MB; the
      swap takes 30-49 ms at 5,000 rows a shard and 75-192 ms at 150,000 (it grows with the shard: Close flushes the
      memtable and writes the open image).
    - writable reopen of the old hot shard at the hot to warm rotation, with the warm memtable: day 6 live 609.6 to
      245.0 MB (the hot and reference shards stay 86.5 MB each), but each rotation allocs +24.7 MB (127.7 against
      103.0), peaks +25 MB (305.3 against 280.1) and stalls 45-55 ms at 5,000 rows and 153-213 ms at 150,000 under
      ts.mu.Lock; and a lock-free warm checkout reads es.store unsynchronised, so a real swap needs the move gate and a
      drain of the pins outside ts.mu (a new lifecycle step, not a config knob).
 4. Peak RSS barely moves with the arena in these runs (a fresh arena is untouched zero pages: RSS 34-70 MB against
    a 177-610 MB heap in section A's baseline). Hypothesis, not measured: the consumer's +106 MB RSS comes from arenas
    that reuse freed heap, which the allocator must zero.
 5. The price of a small WarmMemTableSize (section B, 600 x 100 KB rows into a window shard made on demand): 94-120 MB/s
    at 0 (64 MB), 32, 16 and 8 MB alike (host noise included); the shard ends in 1, 2, 4 and 4-8 tables.
