`go test ./...` with -p 4 on 4 vCPU, so these three share the CPUs with badger, tiered and index; memory alone
237 s vs 300 s next to badger/tiered here, and in CI (all of them together) each of the three crossed Go's
600 s default package timeout (run 38355789742). Root cause: package concurrency on 4 vCPU plus a few
oversized -short -race oracles. Fix: one CI job per race part (03) and size cuts of the longest tests (02).
