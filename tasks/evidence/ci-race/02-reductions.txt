Guards that still pass at the smaller size (after-runs PASS above and in the full package runs):
- Retraction: retractions > 0 and retractions next to an earlier life > 0 over all backends.
- ET pointwise: per seed, "compaction trimmed versions but no pin refused" check; every entity x pin compared.
- CurrentStamps: every op kind (updateNode ... updateInPlace) succeeded and above-head / deleted / revived
  coverage reached, per backend, at 8 seeds.
- RelSegments: per seed sealed, superseded sealed rows, corrections and close+delete steps all taken.
- Memory cursor: 2000 IDs = 3 full pages of 512 + a short tail; pageSize 1 and 1<<20 edges kept. The pageSize=1
  walk is quadratic (each page re-sorts the whole ID set), hence the -short size.

Tried and reverted: TestTxBackfillOracle_CrossBackend 24 -> 8 seeds. Its generator-coverage check failed
(12 door/op kinds never accepted); the smallest passing count is 24 (non-race sweep: 8:12 missing, 10:8,
12:6, 14:6, 16:5, 18:5, 20:3, 22:3, 24:0). It stays at 24 seeds (~70 s alone); sharding carries it.

Not cut: memory TestSegments_* (layouts and row counts are the cases: seal boundaries, byte growth per
